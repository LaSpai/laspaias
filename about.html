About

Lazaros Spaias is a photographer from Thessaloniki, Greece, currently living in the Netherlands. His work focuses on street/documentary photography, drawn to subcultures, communities, and the margins of everyday life. Street life in Amsterdam, Utrecht and Athens runs through much of his work, with an eye for the different, the non-mainstream, and the often overlooked.

Shaped by street culture, he came to photography in 2025 after a rock climbing injury grounded him. He picked up a camera and never put it down using photography as a way to document community, preserve memory, and capture the honest tension of people who resist easy categorisation.
